# Primary Maths — Explanation Sub-Levels (`9 → 1`) (`primary/maths/levels.md`)

- **`Level 9 → 7`:** Standard KS2 expected working (`columnCalc`, `barModel`, inverse arithmetic, mixed fractions) + Sense Check.
- **`Level 6 → 4`:** Step-by-step place-value partitioning (`Hundreds | Tens | Ones`), `✗ vs ✓` place-value/borrow traps, and `fractionBar` visual strips.
- **`Level 3 → 1`:** `numberLine` jumps, `tenFrame` / place-value counters, and concrete real-world object sharing (`counters`).
