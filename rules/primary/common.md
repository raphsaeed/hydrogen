# Primary / KS2 (Ages 7–11, Years 3–6) — Product-Wide Common Rules (`primary/common.md`)

> **Scope:** Shared across **Primary (KS2)** vaults. Strictly isolated from 11+ Algebra-First and Secondary rules.

- **No Algebraic $x$ at `Level 9`:** Unlike `gyaraplus`, Primary (KS2) solutions use **Bar Models, Inverse Operations (`functionMachine`), Mixed Fractions, and Column Arithmetic** as the primary `Level 9` method—avoiding formal algebraic equations unless a Year-6 SATs missing-number topic specifically tests simple symbols.
- **Friendly KS2 Tone:** Warm, clear classroom language suitable for ages 7–11.
