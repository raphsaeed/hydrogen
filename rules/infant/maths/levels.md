# Infant Maths — Explanation Sub-Levels (`9 → 1`) (`infant/maths/levels.md`)

- **`Level 9 → 7`:** KS1 number sentence (`4 + 3 = 7`) paired with `tenFrame` or part-whole cherry model.
- **`Level 6 → 4`:** `numberLine` counting-on hops (`Start at 4, jump +1, +1, +1 -> 7`) and `✗ vs ✓` counting-from-start mistake check.
- **`Level 3 → 1`:** Concrete grouped `counters` / illustrated apples, fingers, and blocks counted aloud one by one (`1, 2, 3...`).
