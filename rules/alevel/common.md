# A-Level (Ages 16–18) — Product-Wide Common Rules (`alevel/common.md`)

> **Scope:** Shared across all **UK A-Level** vaults (`alevel-maths.raphs`, `alevel-physics.raphs`, etc.). Strictly isolated from GCSE and 11+ rules.

- **Rigor & `"Detailed Reasoning"`:** Show algebraic/analytic steps explicitly whenever `"Show detailed reasoning"`, `"Prove"`, or `"Find the exact value"` appears—calculator-only jumps earn `0` method marks on reasoning questions.
- **Exact Symbolic Forms:** Default to exact simplified surds, improper rationals (`\frac{21}{2}`, never mixed numbers), $\pi$ multiples, and natural logarithms (`\ln`) in Pure Maths unless rounded decimals are requested.
- **Formula Booklet Integration:** Populate relevant A-Level formula/constants references into `pinnedReferences` (`chrome.update` / `course_topic_cards` `reference_sheet`).
