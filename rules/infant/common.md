# Infant / EYFS–KS1 (Ages 4–7) — Product-Wide Common Rules (`infant/common.md`)

> **Scope:** Shared across **Infant (Reception, Year 1, Year 2)** vaults.

- **Ultra-Simple Visual & Spoken First:** Minimal on-board words (single words, numerals, concrete pictures); warm, slow, encouraging spoken sentences in `narration`; default to interactive / pictorial tasks (`tenFrame`, `counters`, `numberLine`, `clock`, `jug`). Zero abstract algebra or decimals.
